THE PROBLEM

39.6 million small businesses in Nigeria can’t buy marketing they can trust

They sell on Instagram and WhatsApp. Here’s how they get marketing done today:

Pay a freelancer first

Found on WhatsApp or Instagram. No refund if the work is late, bad or never arrives.

Do it themselves

A flyer on Canva after closing time. Hours away from selling, and it shows.

Hire an agency

Out of reach. A $1–$15 job is too small for agencies and freelance marketplaces.

AI can do this work for a few dollars, but not on its own. Owners have no crypto wallet or USD card, and agents that spend money need limits they can’t talk their way past.

OUR BIGGEST BLOCKER

Naira in, USDC out, at scale.

Most customers pay in naira by bank transfer. NairaDesk funds their USDC escrow on Arc from a float I top up by hand, and naira refunds are manual. Demand isn’t the limit. The float is.

A naira ramp

An on/off-ramp that settles naira straight to USDC on Arc, and back for refunds.

Gas sponsorship

So customers and agents never have to hold USDC for gas.

Working float

Enough USDC to keep jobs flowing while bank transfers settle.
